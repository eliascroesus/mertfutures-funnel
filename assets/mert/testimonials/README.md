# Testimonials

Drop student and community proof in this folder: Discord or DM messages, screenshots of trade journals,
recordings of live sessions, short video clips.

## How to upload

1. Open this folder on GitHub (`assets/mert/testimonials`).
2. Click **Add file → Upload files** and drag the files in.
3. Click **Commit changes**.

## What works best for this market

The buyer has been burned by "gurus" before, so process proof beats profit screenshots:

- Messages from members about executing their plan live, sticking to their rules, or keeping an account.
  "Attempt #N → passed → kept it" stories land best.
- Trade journals showing rules followed (planned trades taken, max-loss breaches before vs. after).
- Unedited recordings of Mert's own sessions, **losses included**.

Avoid P&L, payout and funded-certificate screenshots in ads: Meta rejects them and the FTC treats them as
earnings claims. If one is ever shown on the page, it needs the typical-results context in the footer.

## Files

- **Images:** `.jpg`, `.png` or `.webp`. Screenshots straight from the phone are fine.
- **Videos:** `.mp4`, ideally under 10 MB each so the page stays fast (GitHub's upload limit is 25 MB per file).
- **Names:** lowercase with dashes, e.g. `daniel-first-live-week.png`.

Only use real testimonials you have the person's OK to share. Blur out account numbers, emails and
anything else private before uploading.

New uploads don't appear on the pages by themselves: each one is compressed into `assets/mert/proof/`
(a small `NAME.webp` for the page and a `NAME-full.webp` that opens when tapped) and added to the `PROOF`
list in `index.html`. Keep the originals here. The results sections on the pages stay hidden until `PROOF`
has at least one entry.
